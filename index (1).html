About Our Chapter

The National Action Network – East Metro Atlanta Chapter brings community members together to support civic engagement, advocacy, and equal justice. We work to amplify community voices and encourage meaningful participation in the issues that affect our neighborhoods.

Our Focus

Building a stronger community through action, awareness, and participation.

Justice & Equality

Support efforts that promote fairness, equal opportunity, and accountability.

Community Advocacy

Help residents raise their voices and engage with issues affecting their communities.

Civic Engagement

Encourage informed participation in public life and community decision-making.

Serving East Metro Atlanta

Our chapter’s service area includes communities across the East Metro Atlanta region.

Atlanta DeKalb County Gwinnett County Stone Mountain Lithonia Top of Henry near DeKalb
