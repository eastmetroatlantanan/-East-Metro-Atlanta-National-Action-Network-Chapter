Who We Are

Local voices. Collective action. Lasting impact.

The National Action Network – East Metro Atlanta Chapter is focused on political involvement, advocacy, civic participation, youth leadership, and community service. We aim to create meaningful opportunities for residents to connect, learn, speak up, and take part in issues affecting their communities.

Our chapter welcomes residents, volunteers, community partners, and people who want to contribute their time, ideas, and skills.

Our commitment

Show up. Speak out. Serve.

We work to encourage informed civic participation and build connections across the communities we serve.

  • Promote political involvement and community advocacy
  • Support youth and young adult development
  • Encourage education and career readiness
  • Connect residents through service and community partnerships
Our Work

Focus areas

Our chapter's focus includes civic participation, community advocacy, youth opportunity, and service initiatives.

01 / CIVIC LIFE

Political Involvement & Advocacy

Encourage residents to stay informed, participate in public life, and raise concerns affecting their communities.

02 / YOUTH

Youth & Young Adult Projects

Support leadership development, mentoring, service projects, and opportunities for the next generation.

03 / EDUCATION

School & Education Support

Promote educational enrichment, school support, college awareness, and pathways to continued learning.

04 / CAREER

Career Readiness

Connect young people and adults with information about career exploration, workforce preparation, and opportunity.

05 / COMMUNITY

Community Advocacy

Listen to neighborhood concerns, share information, and support constructive community action.

06 / SERVICE

Community Service & Partnerships

Build relationships with residents, organizations, businesses, and local partners around shared priorities.
